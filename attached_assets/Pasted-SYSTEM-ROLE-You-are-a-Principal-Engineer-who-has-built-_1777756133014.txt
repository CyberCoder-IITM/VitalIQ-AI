── deterioration.py ─────────────────────────────────────────
Implement NEWS2 (National Early Warning Score 2) — the actual
clinical standard used in UK NHS and many US hospitals.

NEWS2 scoring table (implement exactly):

  Respiratory Rate:
    ≤8: +3 | 9-11: +1 | 12-20: 0 | 21-24: +2 | ≥25: +3
  
  SpO2 Scale 1 (no hypercapnic respiratory failure):
    ≤91: +3 | 92-93: +2 | 94-95: +1 | ≥96: 0
  
  Systolic BP:
    ≤90: +3 | 91-100: +2 | 101-110: +1 | 
    111-219: 0 | ≥220: +3
  
  Heart Rate:
    ≤40: +3 | 41-50: +1 | 51-90: 0 | 
    91-110: +1 | 111-130: +2 | ≥131: +3
  
  Consciousness (ACVPU):
    Alert: 0 | Confusion/Voice/Pain/Unresponsive: +3
    (Map GCS: 15=Alert, 13-14=Confusion, <13=Pain/Unresponsive)
  
  Temperature:
    ≤35.0: +3 | 35.1-36.0: +1 | 36.1-38.0: +0 |
    38.1-39.0: +1 | ≥39.1: +2

  Total NEWS2 Score interpretation:
    0: LOW risk
    1-4: LOW risk (monitor every 4-6 hours)
    5-6: MEDIUM risk (urgent review — 1 hour)
    3 in single parameter: MEDIUM risk
    7+: HIGH risk (emergency — continuous monitoring)

Predicts probability of ICU admission using a logistic

                     spo2 < 85 OR
